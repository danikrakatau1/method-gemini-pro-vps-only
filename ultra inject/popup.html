💡 Troubleshooting & Solutions
IMPORTANT "Subscription unavailable / YouTube add-on unavailable"
Cause: Your Google account is still tied to non-US local payment profiles (e.g. local e-wallets) or VPN is leaking, showing local currency instead of US$.
Quick Fix:
  1. Ensure VPN is active on a clean United States (US) server.
  2. Go to payments.google.com → Remove local payment methods.
  3. Set country profile to United States (US) with Oregon ZIP 97201 (tax-free).
  4. Refresh Google One until prices are displayed in US$.
INFO Price on web page shows standard amount
